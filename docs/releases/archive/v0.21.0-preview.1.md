Both desktops now open quota analytics with a practical outlook: quota remaining, estimated time to use it all, and expected unused quota at reset. Five-hour and weekly limits are calculated independently from fresh current-account observations. Historical charts remain in Advanced analysis; unavailable predictions show a reason.
