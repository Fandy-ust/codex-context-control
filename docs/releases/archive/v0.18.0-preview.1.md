## 0.18.0 — Quota indicator & floating controls

- A stepped-bar menu icon replaces the old logo. Bars gradually become hollow as quota decreases; the lower-right label shows the remaining percentage.
- Quota, reset times, extra-credit availability, and chats share one dropdown.
- Sidebar settings open from a bottom-left Control panel button. Click outside to close; default budgets open inside the same compact panel.
- Quotas come from the official runtime, with a five-minute cache and manual refresh. No chat or model-generation request is created.

## 额度指示与悬浮控制面板

- 菜单栏改为阶梯条形图，取代旧 logo。额度减少时条形逐渐空心，右下角显示剩余百分比。
- 额度、重置时间、额外额度和聊天列表共用一个下拉面板。
- 左下角「主控制面板」集中设置，点击外部收起；默认预算在同一紧凑浮窗内打开。
- 使用官方额度接口，五分钟缓存，可手动刷新，不打开聊天、不发起模型生成。

Existing budgets, settings, and caches are preserved. Update in-app or use the DMG below.
