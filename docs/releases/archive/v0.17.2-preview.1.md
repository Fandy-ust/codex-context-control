## Original release notes

## 0.17.2 — Upgrade inheritance and clean installation

- Preserve budgets, defaults, language, switches, caches, and unknown preference fields across updates.
- Keep one lightweight configuration snapshot before a version change. Same-version reinstalls do not duplicate backups.
- Replace old app/code and remove unused runtimes. Keep files that are still in use until their processes exit.
- Retry busy installer-volume detach operations and defer cleanup safely, so downloaded installers do not accumulate.

## 升级继承与旧版本清理

- 预算、默认设置、语言、开关、缓存和未知设置字段原样继承。
- 升级前仅保留一份轻量配置快照，同版本重装不重复生成。
- 旧 App 和代码原位替换，不再使用的运行环境自动清理；仍在使用的文件延后处理。
- 安装镜像繁忙时重试卸载，退出后补做清理，避免 OTA 文件累积。
